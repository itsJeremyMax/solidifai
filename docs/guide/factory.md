Factory is your production hub: the one place to turn a design into a real
object. You set up connections once and use them from any workspace.

## Adding a connection

Open **Factory** from the home header. A connection is a print destination: the
slicer and printer you send work to. Add one with **New connection**, and it is
available across your workspaces from the editor's Make tab.

## Slicers

Factory works with your existing slicer. Point solidifai at the slicer you use in
**Settings**, under Slicers, so estimates and exports match your real setup.

> [!NOTE]
> Connections live at the app level, not inside a single workspace. Set them up
> once in Factory and select from them wherever you are working.

## Estimates and sending

Inside a workspace, open the **Make** tab. Choose a connection and solidifai
slices the current model to report estimated print time and cost, then sends it
on when you are ready.

> [!TIP]
> Assign materials before you estimate. The numbers reflect the material on each
> part, so an accurate model gives an accurate estimate.
